## Opportunity/Gig/Job
1. acquisition:
    . business creates opportunity with clear details
    . business publishes opportunity and can invite bidders
    . Students can place bid, and can accept invite
    . business reviews bid, and accepts offer which automatically moves to or skips interview stage
    . business pays the budget to move to next stage
    . if not skipped, business uploads interview question, or files, or schedules a call interview
    . business moves the bidder to awarded stage or drops them if failed interview [if a budget revision was done on interview, the business updates the offer]

2. Submission and work:
    . student does the work [can be on the side]
    . students submit deliverables for the work
    . business review submitted work at their own time [can only view subitted work, but can't review or action anything if pending budget reviewed in the interview is not payed to zumbarl]
    . business accepts or reverts work [revert can only be upto 3 times]
    . if accepted, student is credited amount
    . business reviews and gives feedback to student
    . opportunity is moved to done

## Marketing
1. acquisition:
    . business creates opportunity [includes details of what level they want, which includes, least number of followers, likes, engagemnts etc, and platforms, and specifies definitation of amount per campaigner]
    . business pays for the marketing campaign
    . business publishes opportunity
    . business invites campaigners, and the campign is only visible to the invites only till the next 24 hours, after which opportunity is open for all [if invites don't reach budget, everyone else still can see it, but can only allow accepts till the budget limit, else after 24 hours, everyone else can now accept]
    . students accept opportunity [can only accept if they pass oportunity criteria]
    . students does the marketing campaign on their socials
    . studenets submit proof of campaign
    . zumbarl creates campaign stats from submitted proofs
    . business views/reviews campign results
    . business endorses top campaigners

## Project
1. acquisition:
    . business creates a project opportunity with clear details, objectives, team roles, learning outcomes, timeline, and expected student commitment
    . business breaks the project into milestones, and every milestone has objectives, deliverables, acceptance criteria, owners, and budget
    . business chooses the team terms they allow: stipend role, attachment, internship, or per-deliverable contributor
    . business opens or closes project team bidding
    . students bid/apply to join the project team under one of the allowed terms
    . zumbarl calculates student pay relative to milestone budget, term, role weight, and zumbarl score
    . business reviews applicants and accepts students into the team

2. milestone setup:
    . business releases milestone funds to zumbarl before the milestone can activate
    . students and business organize the backlog, sprint scope, task owners, deadlines, and check-in cadence
    . zumbarl validates that funding, backlog, sprint plan, owners, and weekly catchups are ready before activation
    . once a milestone is activated, backlog and sprint scope are locked so committed work cannot be removed or expanded silently
    . after lock, the team can move task statuses, comment, upload files, record blockers, and submit evidence, but scope changes need a new change/revision cycle
    . zumbarl auto-creates weekly kanban catchups for planning, blockers, evidence review, and student learning reflection

3. execution and review:
    . students and business work from the kanban/backlog and weekly catchups
    . students submit milestone deliverables, evidence, and training reflections
    . business reviews submitted milestone work in cycles, and can approve or request fixes
    . approved milestone funds are disbursed to students according to calculated pay and contribution term
    . zumbarl records skill growth, portfolio evidence, attachment/internship evidence, business feedback, and payout history
    . the next milestone repeats the same fund-plan-lock-execute-review-disburse cycle

## Learn
1. career selection:
    . student chooses a career ladder or upskill program, for example frontend developer, digital marketer, data analyst, UI designer, or entrepreneur operator
    . student chooses intent: explore, earn while learning, attachment readiness, internship readiness, or job readiness
    . zumbarl creates a baseline profile from current skills, portfolio evidence, completed gigs/projects, posts, endorsements, and student goals
    . zumbarl generates an interactive roadmap tree like roadmap.sh, with levels, checkpoints, resources, practice tasks, recommended gigs, and evidence requirements

2. roadmap execution:
    . student reviews the roadmap and starts tracking progress
    . student can click any checkpoint to see resource recommendations, practice tasks, example projects, mentors/coaches, and matching opportunities
    . student can lock their opportunity feed to the roadmap so recommended gigs, projects, internships, and attachments prioritize the active checkpoints
    . if roadmap lock is active, every Zumbarl interaction such as opportunities, projects, campaigns, posts, reflections, and business reviews updates the related checkpoint score
    . zumbarl creates checkpoint tests and questions, but tests are only 20% of score while 80% comes from verified work, posts, projects, business reviews, and portfolio evidence
    . zumbarl score goes up as checkpoints gain verified evidence

3. transition and verification:
    . when a checkpoint is completed, zumbarl updates the student portfolio and recommends the next work exposure
    . when enough checkpoints are completed, the student moves up a market-ready tier
    . if the roadmap requires external exposure, zumbarl recommends attachment, internship, office tour, mentorship, or transition coaching programs
    . once the full roadmap is completed, zumbarl marks the student verified for that career ladder and adds the credential to the portfolio
    . businesses can see verified students in transition-ready pools and can invite them to interviews, internships, attachments, or structured project teams
    
## zumbarl connect
1. social identity and status:
    . student creates a Connect profile from their campus identity, interests, skills, shop, portfolio, groups, and safety preferences
    . student can publish short status/story updates with text, image/video placeholder, activity context, and optional visibility such as campus, group, or public
    . stories/status updates appear at the top as student icons; a viewer can open a story, react, reply, and click view profile
    . status/story updates expire or archive into the student profile so they build light social proof without becoming permanent spam

2. posts, blogs, tags, and discovery:
    . student creates posts, blogs, images, videos, polls, or project updates
    . viewers can react, comment, save, reshare/repost, report unsafe content, or follow the author
    . every post can include typed tags for a project, product, person, group, club, event, cause, opportunity, or learning roadmap
    . a tagged project opens a side context panel with Zumbarl score, achievements, reviews, feedback, project status, and available actions
    . if the tagged project is open, a viewer can bid; if closed, the viewer can click earn skill and Zumbarl creates a related Learn roadmap
    . a tagged product opens cart, product details, shop owner, and shop page actions
    . a tagged person, group, or club opens profile, follow, message, join, and mutual context actions
    . tags help posts become useful entry points into Earn, Learn, Marketplace, Groups, and Business discovery instead of a disconnected feed

3. groups, clubs, events, and chamas:
    . student can discover or create regulated groups, clubs, class communities, event circles, support circles, and chama-style savings groups
    . group creation requires category, purpose, rules, moderation owner, campus visibility, and safety boundaries
    . students can request to join, accept group rules, contribute posts, attend events, and invite trusted members
    . chamas can define a savings goal, contribution amount, contribution cadence, member list, wallet summary, and contribution ledger
    . group wallets are visible to members, but lending/withdrawal actions require group rules and approval controls
    . events and clubs can publish updates, member stories, attendance calls, and related marketplace/opportunity links

4. safety, quality, and end state:
    . Zumbarl checks posts, stories, groups, and comments for unsafe content, scams, harassment, and spam
    . content with reports enters review; repeat offenders lose posting, group, or marketplace privileges
    . high-quality activity updates the student profile with interests, social proof, useful tags, community roles, and creator signals
    . end state is a useful campus social graph where students can express status, find people, join groups, grow clubs, discover products/projects, and turn closed projects into learning paths

## Market place
1. shop setup and listing:
    . student creates a seller shop with name, category, campus, pickup/drop-off spots, contact rules, return rules, and shop safety terms
    . shop gets a public shop page and shareable handle/subdomain, for example myshop.zumbarl.com
    . shop profile behaves like a seller profile with products, services, promos, shop stories/status, tagged posts, reviews, score, policies, and response history
    . seller can upload products or services with price, stock, condition, variants, delivery options, pickup spots, and proof images
    . every product has its own gallery that the seller can update after publishing without breaking the product page
    . seller can publish posts/blogs/promos related to the shop or product; those appear in Connect as tagged product/shop posts

2. marketplace discovery and buyer checkout:
    . marketplace discovery should be separate from community exploration, using listing/search/category UI instead of a general social feed
    . buyers browse shop stories/status, product listings, services, categories, campus availability, seller scores, and safe pickup rules
    . buyer can open product details, chat/make offer, add to cart, review cart, choose campus pickup or drop-off, pay, and confirm order
    . marketplace supports services as bookable listings with scope, date/time, campus location, and acceptance confirmation

3. seller order fulfilment:
    . seller receives order after checkout and must confirm availability before packaging begins
    . seller moves order through confirmed, packaging, ready for pickup/drop-off, in transit/handoff, delivered, and completed
    . buyer sees order progress, seller confirmation, packaging status, handoff location, transport/drop-off details, and expected pickup window
    . drop-off and pickup are limited to approved campus spots such as library entrance, student center, hostels, and admin block
    . if seller cannot fulfil, Zumbarl routes buyer to refund/replacement flow and flags seller reliability

4. trust, reviews, and end state:
    . buyer reviews item quality, accuracy, handoff, delivery timing, and seller communication
    . seller can review buyer reliability, payment completion, pickup punctuality, and communication
    . seller shop earns Zumbarl score from buyer reviews, delivery quality, response speed, fulfilment accuracy, and dispute history
    . buyer earns Zumbarl score from verified buying, paying on time, completing pickup/drop-off, and leaving useful reviews
    . end state is a trusted campus marketplace where student sellers can run shops and buyers can safely purchase products or services with visible fulfilment progress
