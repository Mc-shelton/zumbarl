# Zumbarl App List (Extracted From Docs)

Sources reviewed:
- `docs/ideation_notes.md`

## Core (MVP / Early Launch)
1. Campus Jobs  
Student paid gigs and part-time opportunities from SMEs.

2. Zumbarl Work  
Scoped skill work (web, coding, graphics, admin) with staged delivery.

3. Zumbarl Marketing  
Student-led promo tasks (social posting, leads, calls, street activation).

4. Errands & Delivery  
Trust-based errands with confirmation checkpoints before payout.

5. Student Marketplace  
Campus storefronts for products and services.

6. Second-Hand Market  
Resale channel for student essentials.

7. Student Services Exchange  
Peer services: printing, laundry, delivery, queueing, and quick tasks.

8. Digital Chamas  
Savings groups, pooled contributions, and internal lending behavior.

9. Budget Helper  
AI budgeting to estimate survival runway from income.

10. Earn-to-Pay Plans  
Pre-pay essentials (food, hostel, supplies) from ongoing earnings.

11. Zumbarl Coaching  
Transition tracks, learning paths, mentorship, and exposure programs.

12. Endorsements & Reputation  
Tiered trust and progression from repeat work and verified outcomes.

13. Student Libraries  
Past papers, notes, and shared learning resources.

14. Groups, Clubs & Pages  
Community spaces for clubs, social coordination, and campus belonging.

15. Wellness & Counseling  
Anonymous support, virtual counseling, and wellbeing programs.

16. Events & Volunteering  
Event listing and volunteer participation across campuses.

## Growth (After Core Adoption)
1. HELB Delay Bridge  
Micro-advance bridge for delayed disbursements.

2. Zumbarl Evergreen  
Continuous internship/attachment recruitment loops with SMEs.

3. Classrooms & Assignments  
Lecturer/student coordination for schedules, announcements, and submissions.

4. Student Affairs Feed  
Council news, classrep updates, and policy communication.

5. Campus Business Compass  
Location and visibility layer for campus vendors/services.

6. Integrations Hub  
WhatsApp/social channels, banking rails, eateries, printing, and partner systems.
