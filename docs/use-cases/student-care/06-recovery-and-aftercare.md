# Recovery Navigation and Aftercare

## SC-RC-01 — Enter recovery support without a treatment claim

1. Open **Recovery Navigation & Staying Well**.
2. Review every step and disclosure.
3. Enroll using synthetic data.

Expected:

- the flow describes care coordination and professional referral;
- it does not promise detoxification, diagnosis, medication or rehabilitation delivered by Zumbarl;
- a named private follow-up and consent record are created.

## SC-RC-02 — Connect professional and peer support

1. Activate the recovery plan as the operator.
2. Provide a visible next step directing the student to an approved professional referral.
3. Optionally join the Recovery & Staying Clean Circle with an alias.

Expected:

- professional referral and peer-circle membership remain distinct;
- joining the peer circle is optional;
- peer members cannot see the private plan, goal or referral;
- the circle states that peer support is not clinical treatment.

## SC-RC-03 — Handle a setback non-punitively

1. Submit **I had a setback** and request follow-up.
2. Inspect the student card, operations queue, profile and score.

Expected:

- all earlier progress remains;
- the care case is prioritized for follow-up;
- there is no lost streak, public badge removal, score reduction, opportunity restriction or accusatory language;
- immediate-danger guidance remains available separately.

## SC-RC-04 — Establish aftercare

1. Advance through the plan steps in a disposable environment.
2. Set a final student-visible aftercare note and future check-in date.
3. Complete the pathway.

Expected:

- the final plan preserves referral and progress history;
- aftercare is visible privately to the student;
- completion resolves the linked request without deleting access to history;
- the student can still request new human support.
