# Support Circles, Sessions and Events

## SC-CI-01 — Review a proposed support circle

1. As a student, create a Wellness circle.
2. Confirm that it is visible only to its creator as **Pending review** and cannot accept members.
3. As Student Affairs, open **Circle reviews**, assign a verified moderation owner and approve it.

Expected:

- student-created support circles never open immediately;
- other students cannot discover or join the pending circle;
- approval records reviewer, note, timestamp and verified moderation owner;
- the creator receives a notification after approval or rejection.

## SC-CI-02 — Join with an alias

1. Join First-Year Peer Support with a generated alias.
2. View the circle as another member.

Expected:

- members see the alias, not the student's profile;
- the joining disclosure explains authorized safeguarding access;
- circle membership does not appear publicly.

## SC-CI-03 — Moderate harmful content

1. Post synthetic content that violates the seeded circle rules.
2. As a circle administrator, remove it.

Expected:

- only authorized circle managers can remove member content;
- system events remain in the circle record;
- removal does not reveal an alias member's profile to ordinary members.

## SC-CI-04 — Schedule a support session

1. As a circle administrator, schedule an audio circle.
2. Enable host approval, members-only access and alias participation.
3. RSVP as a member and request admission.

Expected:

- the session appears in the circle schedule;
- ineligible users cannot enter;
- the host can admit a waiting member;
- Voice Shield and its anonymity limitations are disclosed.

## SC-CI-05 — Publish a suitable event to Explore

1. Schedule a non-sensitive wellbeing event and enable **Publish on Explore Campus**.
2. Inspect Explore as a non-member.

Expected:

- the public event uses the circle identity, not the publishing administrator;
- private membership, aliases, chat and attendance details are absent;
- a sensitive closed session can remain entirely members-only.
