# Zumbarl: The Ecosystem for Campus Talent

## Vision

Zumbarl is a campus life and talent development ecosystem designed to help students learn, earn, grow, and transition successfully into professional careers.

The platform exists to bridge the gap between education and employment while simultaneously solving everyday student challenges around income, career development, mental wellness, campus commerce, and community support.

Our mission is simple:

**Help students graduate with more than a degree. Help them graduate with experience, income, skills, networks, and opportunities.**

---

# Core Pillars

## 1. Learn

Students should have access to structured pathways that help them develop practical skills and become career-ready.

### Features

* Career Roadmaps
* Learning Programs
* Mentorship
* Industry Exposure
* SME Shadowing
* Office Tours
* Skills Certifications
* Career Coaching

Students choose career paths and progress through structured five-stage roadmaps.

Examples:

* Backend Development
* UI/UX Design
* Data Analysis
* Digital Marketing
* Entrepreneurship
* Finance
* Operations

Roadmaps help students understand:

* What to learn
* What projects to complete
* Which opportunities to pursue
* Which companies to engage with

---

## 2. Earn

Students should be able to earn income while building their careers.

### Zumbarl Work

Project-based work for SMEs and businesses.

Examples:

* Graphic Design
* Video Editing
* Social Media Management
* Data Entry
* Research
* Lead Generation
* Website Development
* Administrative Support

### Key Principle

Every completed project contributes to:

* Portfolio growth
* Skill growth
* Career progression
* Trust building

Students can choose between:

* Earn Mode
* Build Career Mode

One optimizes for income.

The other optimizes for long-term professional development.

---

## 3. Build

Students need practical experience before graduation.

### Transition Programs

Businesses can participate in structured transition initiatives.

Examples:

* Internships
* Attachments
* Job Shadowing
* Office Tours
* Mentorship Programs
* Graduate Readiness Programs

### Hiring Guardrails

A business may engage the same student up to three times.

To continue engaging that student, the business must contribute to their development through:

* Mentorship
* Office Exposure
* Coaching
* Shadowing Programs

This creates a healthy balance between work and growth.

---

Students do not adopt cool ideas.

The first version must:

3. Create income
4. Improve opportunities
